Communication job
By the end of the deck, maintainers and project stakeholders should understand what was implemented, how safety was designed, and why code-level release confidence is high: the three selected features are complete, the final audit closed the important boundary cases, and the full regression suite passes.

Audience
Bot maintainers, server administrators, and project stakeholders.

Narrative arc (11 slides)
1. Title — Discord Bot 長期運用基盤 / 実装完了レポート
2. Decision — 4位・5位・6位を一つの運用基盤として実装
3. Architecture — 診断・観測・変更の3層
4. /checkbot — 変更せずに権限とリソースを診断
5. 30-minute reconciliation — 観測と修復を分離
6. /setup — 30分TTLの安全な下書きワークフロー
7. /config — 履歴・差分・適用・ロールバック
8. Fail-safe policy — 不明なら止める、成功を推測しない
9. Final audit — 重大境界条件を9項目で閉じた
10. Verification — 実装者と監督者を分離し、372件で確認
11. Handoff — コードレベルのゲート通過、残るのは実環境スモーク

Design system
- Codex Grid-inspired 1280x720 white canvas.
- Japanese-capable Yu Gothic typography; black ink; pale gray structural panels.
- One deliberate Discord-inspired accent (#5865F2) and a light accent tint.
- Flat composition, thin rules, no decorative gradients, no repeated UI-card dashboard.
- Minimum body size 17px; slide titles 38–42px; deck title 58–64px.
